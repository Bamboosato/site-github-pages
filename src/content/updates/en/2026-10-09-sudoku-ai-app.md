title: "Five difficulty levels, pause, and saved puzzles"

Choose from five difficulty levels: beginner, easy, medium, hard, and expert. Pause the timer and hide the board, then resume play. Use Save to store the current board, pencil notes, elapsed time, and mistake count in your browser. My Puzzles lets you resume, restart, or delete a saved puzzle.
