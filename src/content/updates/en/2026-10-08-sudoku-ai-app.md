Sudoku AI is now listed. It offers three difficulty levels, manual and automatic pencil notes, Undo, erase, reset incorrect entries, local naked-single and hidden-single hints with identification of incorrect entries, and light/dark themes. The Sudoku game and local hints require no account or Gemini API key. Four levels of Gemini advice are available as an optional online feature.
