Added four-category organization and filtering for favorites, category-specific map markers, ordering controls, and pinning. A timeline-linked chart shows JMA rainfall intensity at the map center, and autoplay stops at the final frame. Chart values represent tile color bands, not precise observations at the selected point. The usage notes now also cover location permission when opening the favorites tab.
