title: "Back up and transfer favorite locations"
---

Back up and restore favorites as JSON, or transfer them manually to another device with a QR code or URL. Preview merging or replacing the list, and undo the most recent import. Search ordering and distance labels have been improved, with a vertical rainfall-intensity scale for mobile screens and notices for missing rain data and saved catalogs. Transfer links contain names, coordinates, and viewpoints; anyone with the link can import the list, so choose recipients carefully.
