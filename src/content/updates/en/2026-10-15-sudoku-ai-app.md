title: "Popup number input and app update notifications"
---

Selecting a cell now opens a 3×3 number palette. Use 入力方式 (Input Method) in the menu to switch to the bottom number pad; your choice is saved in the same browser. Tap an entered digit again in the palette to erase it, including a digit whose remaining count is zero when it is already in the selected cell or its pencil notes. A notification and update button have also been added for when a new version is detected.
