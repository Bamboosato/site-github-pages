title: "Automatically select imported notes"
---

After importing Markdown or text files as new notes, the first successfully saved note is now selected automatically. Unsaved changes, unfinished tag input, an in-progress save, or selection/edit/save actions during import prevent automatic selection and preserve the user's current work. The view mode and search/tag filters stay unchanged.
