title: "Two-stage hints and simpler controls"
---

Local hints now guide you from an area to focus on to the target cell and a clue in two stages. The old Gemini adviser, automatic pencil notes, and solve-all buttons have been removed from the controls. Choose one of five difficulty levels from 新規 (New Game), and manually save and resume progress using マイパズルに追加 (Add to My Puzzles) and マイパズル一覧 (My Puzzles) in the menu. Smartphones now use two rows of number keys, with larger board digits and pencil notes.
