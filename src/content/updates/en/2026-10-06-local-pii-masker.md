Improved error handling when saving or deleting encrypted mappings, with information about recovery needs and unreferenced or temporary files. Added incomplete model-cache warnings and an option to remove model assets for downloading again while retaining current work and saved mappings. Linked names must be unlinked before saving a mapping.
