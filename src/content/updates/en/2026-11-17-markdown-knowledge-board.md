title: "Markdown Knowledge Board: existing notes and metadata through Local MCP"
---

After PC setup and an explicit connection, enabling reading and updating in Advanced settings lets Codex search, retrieve, and update existing notes. Retrieved bodies are passed to Codex. The same permission enables conditional Custom metadata Add, Update, Upsert, and Remove after previewing targets and changes. Removal requires a compatible service and app. This does not automatically sync PCs.
