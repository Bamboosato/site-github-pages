title: "Added English-notation stages to Stroop Route"
---

Stroop Route now has seven stages, including English-only Stage 6 and Stage 7 with mixed hiragana, katakana, kanji, and English. English notation is also included in the daily challenge and 7×7 and 10×10 random mazes. The timer, move count, and rule labels are larger, and the stage selector has been consolidated.
